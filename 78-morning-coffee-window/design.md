## Difference from recent person prompts

Changed at least four axes from every recent prompt cluster:
- silhouette: longline shirt + barrel trouser instead of sleeveless dress, lace shell, gilet, or fitted lounge top;
- neckline: clean open spread collar/soft shirt V instead of scoop, sweetheart, square, deep gilet V, or crew mesh;
- sleeves: long sleeves rolled to below elbow instead of sleeveless or short puff sleeves;
- material: cotton-silk fine-stripe broadcloth + matte twill instead of poplin dress, mesh/tulle, lace, knit, or slub suiting;
- layering: one-shirt direction, no sheer overlay or black inner;
- palette: ice-blue/warm-white stripes + pearl grey, avoiding marigold, black, lavender, mineral blue solid, and pale denim;
- bottom: barrel-leg trouser rather than jeans, skirt, dress, or lounge midi;
- accessories: none.

## Physique and neckline

The adult shirt collar frames the clavicles and allows only a subtle partial central line; full secure coverage remains at sides and underside. The cup and arms stay below the bust. Fabric allowance and drape preserve the reference body's outer width, projection, and position without compression.

## Scene connection

The rolled cuffs expose the working hands; the vertical stripes remain readable in the side-front window light; the long shirt and barrel trousers create a relaxed morning line without pajama styling. The cup is intentionally low and small so the person, not coffee still life, remains primary.

## Existing-idea distinction

Unlike generic coffee-only greetings, the image locks one decisive interval: aroma before first sip. It excludes food, books, flowers, phone, and styled breakfast props, and uses the human face, hands, textile, and diffuse light as the complete event.
