# 06 — 夕暮れの境目（現実の人物 × 幻想へ溶ける背景）

人物は画像参照。プロのカメラマンが設定を詰めた写実の人物に対し、背景が距離とともに幻想へ溶けていく構成。「現実と空想の境目」を距離で3層に設計し、継ぎ目は見せない。

- **アスペクト比:** 9:16（全身とスカートの縦の流れ、空のグラデーション）
- **見せ場:** 風に流れるロングスカートの裾。胸の前は空け、体に沿う生地

## 設計メモ

- **人物 = 完全な写真:** 85mm f/1.4 / f/2 / 低ISO、シルバーレフでの起こし、毛穴・産毛・布の織りまで実写指定。`Nothing about her is stylized` で人物側の絵画化を遮断
- **背景 = 3層のグラデーション:** ①足元数メートルは物理的に正確 → ②中景で被写界深度では説明できない滲みが始まり、窓明かりが家からわずかに浮く → ③遠景と空は完全に幻想（提灯の光が宙に浮かぶ、地平線が空に溶ける）
- **境目感:** `NO visible seam` を明記し、どこから幻想かを特定できなくする。全面に均一なフィルムグレインをかけて実写とペイント調を同じ一枚に接着（実写合成でも使う手法）
- 幻想要素はすべて低彩度・低コントラストに抑え、人物が画面で最もシャープで彩度が高い存在になるよう指定

## 英語プロンプト

```text
A highly detailed photorealistic portrait of the person from the reference image.
9:16 aspect ratio. Infer apparent age from the reference image and preserve it.
Match the reference image exactly for gender presentation, ancestry, body shape and
lines, height impression, proportions, overall build, skin tone and texture, facial
features, hair, and all physical characteristics including chest and hip shape and
fullness. Reproduce the natural volume and silhouette of the bust and hips as seen
in the reference, kept accurate through the fit and drape of the clothing. The bust
sits high and supported on the ribcage, as if wearing a well-fitted bra: its fullest
point is level with the mid-upper arm, roughly at armpit height — never sagging low
toward the waist. Preserve the subject's identity and physique faithfully without
age-shifting, beautifying, exaggerating, or reshaping.

Photographic treatment of the subject: she is rendered with the exacting realism of
a professional editorial photograph — shot on a full-frame camera with an 85mm f/1.4
lens, aperture around f/2, ISO kept low for clean skin tones. True-to-life skin
texture with visible pores and fine vellus hair at the jawline, individual hair
strands catching light, natural fabric weave and micro-wrinkles in the clothing.
Her key light is the warm remaining daylight from the horizon, lifted by a subtle
silver reflector from camera-left so her eyes hold a soft catchlight; a cool rim
from the darkening sky separates her hair and shoulders from the background.
Nothing about her is stylized, smoothed, or painted.

Pose: standing at the top of a long stone stairway, body three-quarters toward the
camera, chin slightly lifted toward the last light. One hand gently holds back hair
lifted by an evening breeze; the other rests at her side. Nothing is held in front
of the chest — the torso stays open so the silhouette reads clearly. Calm,
unhurried expression.

Outfit: a deep twilight-blue camisole dress in soft, fluid satin-backed crepe that
follows the body — curving over the bust, draping from its outermost point with
gentle tension lines, falling into a long skirt that streams sideways in the
breeze; the wind-caught hem is the outfit's single focal point. Thin straps, modest
straight neckline, fully lined and opaque. Simple flat sandals.

Background — a gradient from reality into dream, staged by distance:
(1) Near ground, within a few meters of her feet: fully real and physically
accurate — worn stone steps with lichen, a metal railing, sharp believable detail
consistent with the same photograph.
(2) Middle distance: reality begins to loosen. Rooftops and trees soften beyond
what depth of field alone explains — edges feather like wet-on-wet paint, warm
window lights bloom slightly too large and drift a fraction off their houses, thin
mist threads between buildings.
(3) Far distance and sky: fully dreamlike — the town dissolves into an indigo-to-
amber twilight gradient with a painterly, luminous quality; a scatter of lantern
lights rises off the ground and hangs in the air like slow embers, and the horizon
line melts into the sky with no hard edge.
There must be NO visible seam between these zones — the transition is continuous
and gradual, so the eye cannot point to where the photograph ends and the dream
begins. The dream elements stay muted and low-contrast; she remains the sharpest,
most saturated, most real thing in the frame.

Atmosphere and grade: cinematic color grade with gentle film grain across the
whole frame (the grain unifies the real and unreal zones), soft halation around
the distant lights, no HDR look, no oversharpening.

Format: 9:16, vertical composition — she stands full height in the center-left
third, sharp against the dissolving town, the sky gradient filling the upper frame.
```
